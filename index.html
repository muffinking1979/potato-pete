Potato Pete

  • Pete the cat loves potatoes. Press Space to jump and catch them in his mouth as they fly by.
  • A quick tap is a small hop. Hold Space longer to jump higher.
  • Every potato Pete eats scores 100 points and gives back one unit of health.
  • Every potato he misses costs one unit of health.
  • Grab the potatoes glowing gold! A golden potato is worth 300 points.
  • Watch out for potatoes glowing red! A bad potato takes 5 units of health and leaves Pete stuck for a moment.
  • Potatoes keep getting faster, and after a while more of them come than Pete can catch. Pick the ones he can reach.
  • When his health runs out, the game is over.
